# Claim Types — Verification Strategies

Reference guide for claim types and how to verify each one.

---

## 1. LAW (law, regulation)

**Examples:**
- "VASP Act 2024 came into effect September 1, 2024"
- "Section 14.2(10) provides an exemption for miners"

**How to verify:**
1. Find the original text of the law (official government gazette, regulator website)
2. Check number, date, article, paragraph
3. Ensure the quote matches the original

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Official law publication (government gazette, official legal databases)
- 🟢 Regulator website
- 🟡 Law firm analysis (Appleby, Harneys, Maples, etc.)
- 🔴 Blog/wiki (only for initial search)

**Minimum sources:** 1 official

**Red flags:**
- Law not found in official databases
- Article number doesn't match
- Effective date differs

---

## 2. NUMBER (amount, rate, cost)

**Examples:**
- "Capital requirement: USD 100,000"
- "0% corporate tax until 2066"
- "Application fee: $70,000"

**How to verify:**
1. Find on regulator website (fee schedules, capital requirements)
2. Check in legal guides (law firm analyses)
3. Compare at least 2 sources (numbers often differ)

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Regulator fee schedule
- 🟢 Official circular / guidance
- 🟡 Law firm (recent analysis, last 2 years)
- 🔴 Outdated source (>2 years old)

**Minimum sources:** 2

**Red flags:**
- Number not found on regulator website
- Different sources give different numbers (note the range)
- Number is outdated (law changed)

---

## 3. DATE (date, deadline, timeline)

**Examples:**
- "Effective date: September 1, 2024"
- "Transition deadline: December 31, 2024"
- "Timeline: 4-9 months"

**How to verify:**
1. Find official announcement (press release, gazette)
2. Check exact date (don't confuse enacted vs effective)
3. For timelines — find real cases (how long it took others)

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Government gazette / official announcement
- 🟢 Regulator press release
- 🟡 News article (with date)
- 🔴 Undated source

**Minimum sources:** 1 official

**Red flags:**
- Confusion between enacted date vs effective date
- Deadline already passed (check if extended)
- Timeline too optimistic / pessimistic

---

## 4. EVENT (incident, precedent)

**Examples:**
- "Garantex sanctioned by OFAC in March 2024"
- "BVI implemented UK Russia sanctions via Overseas Territories Order"

**How to verify:**
1. Find primary source (OFAC press release, government announcement)
2. Check date, participants, details
3. Find follow-up (consequences, current status)

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Official sanctions list / press release (e.g., treasury.gov)
- 🟢 EU/UK sanctions list
- 🟡 Reuters, Bloomberg, CoinDesk (reputable media)
- 🟡 Chainalysis / Elliptic reports
- 🔴 Twitter / social media (only for discovery)

**Minimum sources:** 2

**Red flags:**
- Event not found in official records
- Date differs
- Details distorted (company name, jurisdiction)

---

## 5. COMPANY (company, registry)

**Examples:**
- "Cronex Ltd licensed in AIFC for DATF + Custody"
- "100+ VASP licenses issued in Seychelles"

**How to verify:**
1. Check in official registry (regulator database)
2. Find specific license (number, date, scope)
3. Check current status (active, revoked, suspended)

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Regulator public registry (FSC, FSA, AFSA public registers)
- 🟢 Company's own disclosure (licensed as...)
- 🟡 Industry directories
- 🔴 Self-reported (company website only)

**Minimum sources:** 1 official registry

**Red flags:**
- Company not found in registry
- License status = revoked / suspended
- License scope doesn't match claim

---

## 6. QUOTE (direct quote)

**Examples:**
- Direct quotes from laws
- Quotes from regulatory guidance
- Quotes from analysts/firms

**How to verify:**
1. Find the original document
2. Compare quote word-for-word
3. Check context (not taken out of context)

**Sources:** Original document ONLY

**Minimum sources:** 1 (original)

**Red flags:**
- Quote not found in original
- Quote differs (even slightly)
- Quote taken out of context (meaning changes)

---

## 7. PROCESS (procedure, workflow)

**Examples:**
- "Application takes 4-9 months"
- "Must appoint resident director"
- "Physical office required"

**How to verify:**
1. Find official guidelines (application procedures)
2. Find real experiences (how long it actually took)
3. Check if requirements changed recently

**Sources (by priority):**
- 🟢 Regulator guidelines / application instructions
- 🟡 Law firm practical guides
- 🟡 Testimonials from licensed companies
- 🔴 Generic "offshore company" sites

**Minimum sources:** 2

**Red flags:**
- Process described is outdated (requirements changed)
- Timeline is from marketing material (overly optimistic)
- Requirements listed don't match current rules

---

## 8. COMPARISON (between entities/costs)

**Examples:**
- "Cheaper than Seychelles ($12K vs $340K)"
- "Fastest jurisdiction to set up (2 weeks vs 12 months)"
- "Only jurisdiction without VASP licensing"

**How to verify:**
1. Verify BOTH sides of comparison independently
2. Check if comparison is fair (same scope, same year)
3. Ensure no cherry-picking (show full picture)

**Sources:** Sources for BOTH comparands

**Minimum sources:** 2+ (one per side)

**Red flags:**
- One side verified, other not
- Comparing different scopes (apples to oranges)
- Cherry-picking (ignoring disadvantages)
- Outdated data for one side

---

## Verification Priority Matrix

| Type | Priority | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **number** | 🔴 HIGHEST | Wrong costs/tax rates = wrong business decisions |
| **law** | 🔴 HIGHEST | Wrong legal status = illegal operations |
| **date** | 🔴 HIGH | Wrong dates = missed deadlines |
| **event** | ⚠️ MEDIUM | Precedents inform risk, but don't drive decisions |
| **company** | ⚠️ MEDIUM | Registry checks are quick |
| **quote** | ⚠️ MEDIUM | Important for legal arguments |
| **process** | 🟡 LOWER | Processes change, approximate is OK |
| **comparison** | 🟡 LOWER | Derived from verified individual facts |
